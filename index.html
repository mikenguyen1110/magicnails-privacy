Privacy Policy — Nails Magic (Client Tracker)

Effective date: September 19, 2026
Developer: Mike Nguyen
Contact: mikenguyen1011@gmail.com

Nails Magic ("the App") helps nail technicians keep track of clients, appointments, tickets, and income. This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is treated.

The short version

  • The App works offline. Your data is stored only on your device.
  • We do not collect, upload, sell, or share your data.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.
  • We do not have user accounts.

Information you enter in the App

You may enter the following information. It is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData) and is never sent to the developer or any third party:

  • Client information: first and last name, phone number, notes, service type, and visit dates.
  • Business information: service types and prices, extra charges, tickets, tips, and coworker names.
  • Payroll information: names, date of birth, Social Security number (if you choose to enter it), employment type, and commission percentage.
  • App preferences: your chosen language.

You control this data. You can edit or delete it at any time inside the App. Deleting the App removes all of its data from your device.

Network use

The only network request the App makes is the Software Update check. It sends your app's bundle identifier to Apple's public iTunes Lookup service (itunes.apple.com) to find the latest App Store version. No personal information or client data is included. Apple's handling of that request is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Backup, export, and import

The Data screen lets you export a backup file and import one. Backups are saved wherever you choose (for example, the Files app or iCloud Drive). If you store a backup in a third-party or cloud service, that service's privacy policy applies. Backup files contain the information listed above, including any payroll details you entered, so please store them securely.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information. The App has no third-party SDKs. If you use the share button to send a user manual, or tap the contact email link, that action is handled by iOS and the app you choose.

Children's privacy

The App is intended for business use by adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data security

Your data remains in the App's storage on your device and is protected by your device's security features (passcode, Face ID/Touch ID, and iOS encryption). Keep your device locked and backed up. Because we never receive your data, we cannot recover it if your device is lost and no backup exists.

Your rights

Because all data stays on your device, you already have full access and control: view, change, export, or delete it in the App at any time. Since the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to retrieve or delete on our side.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email mikenguyen1011@gmail.com.
